How it works

  1. Learn a few words. Practise a handful of new words every day.
  2. Open the map. Explore a word's relatives and add the ones you like to your list.
  3. Review. Daily review brings each word back at the right time.

Trial and membership

WordMapp is used with a membership. You try it free for 7 days after signing up; after that you pick a Monthly, Yearly or Lifetime plan. All levels (A1–C2), the maps, the Slang deck and background listening review come with the membership.
