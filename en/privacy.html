Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This text reflects the app's current features. Before release, fill in the developer contact below and have it reviewed legally.

In short

WordMap asks only for an email address to create an account, which is used to store your progress in the cloud. It shows no ads and does not track you.

Data we collect

  • Your email address: to create your account, let you sign in and contact you about your account.
  • Your learning data: progress, review history, visited maps, name and language preferences, and Premium status. It is stored in the cloud so it can be backed up and synced between devices.

We do not use this data for advertising, sell it, or share it with third parties for marketing. Account and data storage run on a secure cloud provider (Supabase); data is encrypted in transit.

Microphone and speech recognition

For pronunciation practice, the microphone and your device's speech recognition service are used only if you grant permission. Audio is not recorded or stored by us. On Android, speech recognition may be processed by Google's speech service, which is subject to Google's own privacy policy. You can revoke the permission any time in device settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

Internet and third parties

  • Photos: Photos on word cards are loaded from Pexels servers. When an image is requested, Pexels can see your device's IP address. Offline, a drawn picture is shown instead.
  • Purchases: Premium purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Children

WordMap does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Deleting your account and data

You can ask us to delete your account and all data stored in the cloud at any time by writing to medclaude1773@gmail.com from your account email. We will complete the request within a reasonable time (at most 30 days). Uninstalling the app does not delete your cloud account. See the Delete Account page for the steps.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
