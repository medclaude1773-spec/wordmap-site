Delete Account

This page explains how to request deletion of your WordMap account and data.

How to delete your account

  1. Send an email to medclaude1773@gmail.com from the email address you used to create the account.
  2. Use "WordMap account deletion request" as the subject. You do not need to add anything else.
  3. We confirm the request came from your account email. We may ask a short verification question if needed.
  4. We complete the request within 30 days at most and confirm by email.

Data that is deleted

  • Your email address and account
  • Your learning data in the cloud: progress, review history, visited maps, name and language preferences, Premium status record

Data that is kept

We keep no data about your account unless a legal obligation requires it. Payment records for Premium purchases are held by Google Play and are outside our control.

Important

Deletion cannot be undone. Uninstalling the app does not delete your cloud account; follow the steps above to delete it.

For more information, see the Privacy Policy.
